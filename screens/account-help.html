Words used in TwinScape
Readback
A value the instrument measures about itself, for example a temperature or a vacuum.
Key readback
The one readback that says most about an instrument right now. TwinScape picks it.
Warning and Alarm
A readback has left its limits. Warning is the first limit, Alarm the second.
Not reporting
The instrument has stopped sending data to TwinScape.
Watchlist
Your own list of instruments you want to see together.
