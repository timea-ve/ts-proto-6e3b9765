How to connect an instrument

Help
1
Check the TwinScape Service
The TwinScape Service is a small program on the instrument's acquisition PC. It sends the data. It must be installed and running.
2
Find the serial number
It is on the type plate of the instrument and in the instrument software.
3
Add the instrument in TwinScape
Open Add instrument, type the serial number and choose Look up. You can give the instrument a name and put it in a watchlist.
Add instrument
arrow_forward
4
Wait for the first data
Readbacks arrive every hour. Until the first ones arrive, the instrument shows as Not reporting.
The instrument does not show up?
If TwinScape does not find the serial number, or no data arrives after one day, contact Bruker Service.
