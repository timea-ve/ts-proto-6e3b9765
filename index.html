TwinScape · Desktop exploration

Clickable HTML/CSS prototype built from the Paper page “10 Desktop exploration” (68 screens, 1440 px). Design evidence only, not production code.

Click buttons, tabs, rows and menu items to move between screens. Press H or use Hotspots in the bottom bar to show clickable areas; [ ] step through screens; Esc closes a menu or dialog, or returns here. Clicking an inactive area briefly flashes the hotspots.
